# The intro: "Arthur Had a Feeling"

An in-game cutscene (about 100 s) that runs straight into gameplay. Pick **Menu → Modes & toys → The intro**. Stand somewhere open
and flat, like a meadow, a field or a riverbank (not in Saint Denis: it won't start there), and look the way you want the storm to
come from. The camp is built right where you stand.

**The premise:** Arthur knew the storm was coming. He's standing in a hot air balloon at the edge of camp before anyone else has
noticed a cloud, and he tells them so, in his way. The gang has opinions. Then the tornado arrives, and he pulls the burner and
leaves them to it.

## How it got here
- **v1.3** (playtest 11: "I couldn't hear any of the characters", "we want to splice the character's actual lines"): every line
  is the characters' own voice with the game's own subtitles, the gang goes up as real ragdolls, and there's no colour grade.
- **v1.4** (playtest 12: "more obvious what's going on... fix its current angles"):
  - a camera director, so a shot that can't see its subject swings round, lifts or pulls back;
  - Arthur filmed from above the basket's rim;
  - a setup that tells the story.
- **v1.5** (playtest 13: "super funny now and readable... give ample time for the 'ears' of the game camera... a little more
  long... a few more key characters, lines, back and forth and jokes... he should explain why he's leaving... hero shot"):
  - about 100 s, with Hosea, John and Lenny;
  - 20 real lines as back-and-forths, each line in its own shot of its speaker;
  - the ears (below), the funnel's standoff, and the hero shot.
- **v1.6** (playtest 14: "nearly perfect... tighten up and use more close up camera angles... less T-posing... that magic shot of
  the gang getting sucked up... arthur should fly into micah"):
  - every close-up of Arthur is in front of his face, and John, Uncle and Hosea face the camera too;
  - Micah runs for the basket and the balloon knocks him flat as it takes off;
  - the gang going up is shot from the camp's edge, low and close;
  - Dutch's slow-motion pass is right across the frame;
  - Arthur's own story-mode gestures (the old Online emotes never loaded, and held the screen black for 6 s);
  - Pearson ladles his stew and Lenny sits calmly in the air (no more T-posing);
  - the thunder is quieter, and the balloon's jets are smaller in the close-ups.
- **v1.6.1** (playtest 15: "overall pretty great and hilarious"):
  - the swoop: the balloon skims across the camp into Micah, then climbs away;
  - the title's boom and the lift-off whoosh are quieter;
  - the camera hands back looking the way he escapes;
  - the riders in the air animate, and Dutch really crosses the slow motion.

After the handoff the funnel keeps circling the camp for 40 s, then your Movement setting takes over. The gang is thrown out by
the tornado itself. They're unhurt; each one gets up, grumbles and walks it off.

## The ears
The game hears from the camera, so:
- **Every line is in its own shot of its speaker,** close to the lens.
- **A line never starts in the first second after a cut.** The game's listener needs a moment to catch up.
- **A cut waits for a line still being said:** up to 2.2 s at a cut, 8 s in all. Never at the cuts round Dutch's pass: he's
  thrown on the scene's clock and flies in real time, so a held cut would let him sail past before the slow motion.
- **A far-off speaker is played from beside the camera,** in their own voice. Camp lines use their audio name; ambient lines use
  the character's voice.

The log shows all of it: `INTRO ... STORY`, `played beside the camera`, `holding the cut`.

## The lines
**Story lines** are the characters' real lines, each played by name as one line of one of Rockstar's own conversations. The
words are exact. They come from camp scenes and from missions; Uncle's lumbago line proved in playtests 12 and 13 that mission
lines play in free roam. The research is in `..\research\intro_v15_lines.md`, including who says what and how sure that is.
If the game won't play a story line (nothing is playing 1.2 s later), an ambient line in the same spirit plays instead.

**Ambient lines** come from the characters' ambient voice banks.

The game shows its own subtitle for every line. The game's Subtitles option needs to be on.

## Good to know
- **Skip:** Backspace or B jumps to Lenny's last word (94.5 s). Pressed again, it goes straight to the handoff.
- **If it's interrupted** (Arthur dies, Despawn everything, a story reload), you get your controls, camera, time speed, HUD,
  weather and settings back.
- **Your horse** is moved 34 m behind the camp before the scene starts, and the tornado leaves it alone until the scene is over.
- **The look:**
  - a stormy THUNDER sky at 17:00, with the rain off and no colour grade (the mod's own random lightning waits till it's over);
  - `[Intro] Grade` can add a grade, and `Hour` changes the time.
- **Outfits:** `[Intro] Outfits=Story` puts everyone back in camp clothes.
- **The log** names every shot the camera director had to move, and anything slow to load.
